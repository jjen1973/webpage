    About

    Frontend development is the practice of converting data to a graphical interface, through the use of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, so that users can view and interact with that data. The challenge associated with front end development is that the tools and techniques used to create the front end of a website are constantly changing and evolving. This means that front end developers must be constantly learning and adapting to new technologies and techniques in order to stay current in their field.
